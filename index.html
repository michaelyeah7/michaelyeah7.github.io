Flagship · 2026 – now

DREAM Brain

A1 · AI × Biology × Robotics

An AI scientist is not a bigger model on the same data. It is a model trained on decisions.

Lab automation already moved execution to machines. Judgement did not move: why an experiment exists, which alternatives were rejected, what outcome was expected. That reasoning stays in someone's head and is gone after the round.

DREAM Brain is the intelligence layer that sits in the middle of the design–build–test–learn loop. It reads the literature, proposes protein designs, commits predictions before the plate goes in, drives the robots and instruments, and learns from the results. Every step is written down as Scientific Process Data, which becomes the training signal for the next, better version of the system.

Embodied AI · 2025

DreamTacVLA

B2 · Robotics

Vision-language-action models generalize well but cannot feel contact. DreamTacVLA gives a VLA high-resolution tactile images as a "micro-vision" stream, aligns them with what the cameras see, and adds a tactile world model. In a Think–Dream–Act loop the policy drafts an action, imagines its contact outcome, and refines the action before moving.

Guo Ye, Zexi Zhang, Xu Zhao, Shang Wu, Haoran Lu, Shihan Lu, Han Liu · Learning to Feel the Future: DreamTacVLA for Contact-Rich Manipulation

+22.3%over tactile-alignment-only and dream-only ablations
